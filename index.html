About Volga Life

Quality, careand value.

At Volga Life Biosciences LLP, we’re building brands that bring quality, care and innovation closer to people. Born in Kolkata, our journey spans aquatics, aquarium products, pet care and beyond, with a simple promise: authentic products, dependable services and value for money.

Since 2018, our flagship brand, A Glass Box®, has been transforming the aquarium hobby in Kolkata and reaching enthusiasts across India, with deliveries extending beyond the country. We believe quality shouldn’t come at an unreasonable price, which is why we’re committed to offering genuine products and budget-friendly solutions for hobbyists and B2B businesses alike.

And we’re only getting started! Our vision is to expand into pet care, aquascaping, professional services and more.
